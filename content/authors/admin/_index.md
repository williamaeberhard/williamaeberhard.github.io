## About Me

I obtained my PhD in Statistics in 2015 in a cotutelle between the [University of Geneva](https://www.unige.ch/gsem/en/research/institutes/risis/) and the [University of Sydney](https://sydney.edu.au/). I then worked as a post-doctoral fellow at [Dalhousie University](https://www.dal.ca/faculty/science/math-stats.html) as part of a Canadian Statistical Sciences Institute collaborative research team. I was an Assistant Professor of Statistics and Coordinator of the MS in Data Science in the [Department of Mathematical Sciences](https://www.stevens.edu/schaefer-school-engineering-science/departments/mathematical-sciences) at Stevens Institute of Technology before joining the [Swiss Data Science Center](https://datascience.ch), affiliated with ETH Zurich, in 2020 as Senior Data Scientist.

My research interests lie in spatio-temporal modeling with applications in ecology, geosciences, and atmospheric sciences, as well as in robust and computational statistics. I have collaborated in various cross-disciplinary teams, including in marine biology, volcanology, and hydrology.
